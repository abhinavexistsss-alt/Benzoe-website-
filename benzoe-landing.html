The everyday problem

For many Indian clinics, the front desk is still a register, a pen, and guesswork.

India has 1M+ independent clinics still stuck in this system. Doctors are overloaded, staff keep repeating answers, and patients wait without clarity.

🗒️

Paper registers and handwritten queues

Walk-in patients keep asking, “mera number kab aayega?” Staff search pages while the waiting area gets louder.

💊

Illegible prescriptions lost after every visit

A patient goes home with a paper slip. Next month, the doctor has no clear history and the patient has misplaced the old prescription.

💸

Cash-only payments with no digital records

Consultation fees, medicines, and receipts often stay outside a clean digital trail — difficult for clinics and patients.

This is not a small inconvenience. It is daily operational chaos across Tier-2 cities, neighbourhood clinics, and busy hospital OPDs.
